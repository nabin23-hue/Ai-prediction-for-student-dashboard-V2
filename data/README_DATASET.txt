Synthetic Training Dataset

File: synthetic_training_dataset.csv
Rows: 700
Purpose: Demonstrates Random Forest training workflow for the ITS320 capstone prototype.

Input columns:
- attendance
- study_hours
- assignments
- quiz
- lms_logins
- engagement
- missed_tasks

Target/output columns:
- productivity
- pass_probability
- risk
- expected_grade

Important:
This dataset is synthetic and should be explained as prototype/demo data only. It is not real student data.
Future work should replace this with UCI Student Performance Dataset, OULAD, or de-identified institutional data after ethical approval.
